The problem

Everyone talks to AI all day. All of it happens in a chat box.

⏳

You can't tell when your agents need you.

Agents run in the background. You keep switching windows to see what's done, what's stuck, and what's waiting for you.

🧾

Physical things are hard to hand to AI.

Receipts, whiteboards, files on a coworker's phone: photograph, upload, copy-paste, every time.

⚠️

Clicking "Approve" is too easy.

Agents now send emails and move money. One careless click can't be undone.

    How it works

    Nibble is the face and hands. The brain stays on your computer and in the cloud.

    🤖NibbleMic, speaker, camera, face screen, turning head, LED ring, touch-sensitive head, NFC nose, little printer
    ⇄
    💻Companion appThe avatar on your laptop, access to your files, the approval queue, plus a phone app for tap-to-send
    ⇄
    🧠Your AI toolsClaude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, plus email, calendar, drive and Slack

    Nibble is a working name. Everything on this page is a simulation of the planned hardware.
